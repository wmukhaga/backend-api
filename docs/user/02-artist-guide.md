# Artist Guide: Registering for an Event

An event coordinator adds you to an event using your email address. You then get an email with a link. You do not need a password.

## 1. Open your link

1. Find the email from TikoSorted. The subject mentions the event.
2. Click the button in the email, or paste the link into your browser.
3. The page says **CHECKING YOUR LINK…** for a moment.

The link is valid for **72 hours** and works **once**. If you see **LINK NOT VALID**, the link has expired or has already been used. Contact your event coordinator.

> **Current limitation:** the app cannot send a second link. Adding the same email to the same event again is refused, so the coordinator needs the technical team to issue a new link.

## 2. Fill in the registration

The **ARTIST REGISTRATION** page has two parts.

1. **Your preferences.** Write what the organisers need to know, for example stage plot, sound requirements and travel notes. This box is required.
2. **Your entourage.** For each band member, dancer or crew member, enter **ENTOURAGE NAME** and **ENTOURAGE EMAIL**, then click the add button to add another person. You can remove a person you added by mistake. You can submit with no entourage.

When you are done, click the submit button. The page shows **SUBMITTING REGISTRATION…** and then **Registration received**. Your details have been saved.

You have about **2 hours** to finish after opening the link. If the page stops working, contact your coordinator.

## 3. Your entourage confirms their email

Every person you add gets an email from TikoSorted saying you added them. They click the link in that email (valid 72 hours) to confirm that the email address is theirs.

## What happens next

The coordinator sees you on the event's artist list, marked **VERIFIED** once you opened your link (until then it says **VERIFICATION PENDING**).
