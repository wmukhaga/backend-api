# FAQ, Troubleshooting and Glossary

## For ticket buyers

**I did not get the M-Pesa prompt.**
Check the number you entered (Safaricom or Airtel, for example 0712 345 678). Make sure your phone has signal and M-Pesa is working. Press **CANCEL** and start again. Your first booking expires on its own.

**The screen says "Booking expired. Please try again."**
Your seats were held for a few minutes and the time ran out before payment. Go back to the event and buy again. If you paid and still got this message, contact support with your M-Pesa receipt before paying again.

**It says "Payment timed out".**
We did not receive your payment in time. Try again. If M-Pesa took your money, keep the M-Pesa message and contact support.

**It says "Not enough tickets available".**
Someone bought the last tickets, or the event is no longer on sale. Reduce the quantity or choose another ticket type.

**My ticket page says NO TICKETS YET.**
Your payment may still be processing. Wait a minute and refresh. If it is still empty after several minutes, contact support.

**I did not get an email.**
Check spam. You can always open your ticket from the link in the email. If you lost it, contact support with your phone number and M-Pesa receipt.

**Can I get a refund?**
Charges are not refundable once the ticket is issued.

**Can I buy VIP and Regular together?**
Not in one order. Make two orders.

**The gate says my ticket is already used.**
Each QR works once. Someone may have a copy of your ticket. Go to the help desk with your ID and M-Pesa receipt.

## For artists

**My link says LINK NOT VALID.**
The link is older than 72 hours or has already been used. Contact your event coordinator.

**Do I have to add my entourage now?**
No. Preferences are required, entourage is optional.

## For staff

**I cannot log in.**
Check your email and password. If you were logged in on another device, you were signed out there. There is no password reset screen yet, so ask a manager.

**I was logged out suddenly.**
Sessions last 8 hours, closing the tab signs you out, and logging in on another device ends this session.

**A page is empty or shows a generic error.**
Your role may not allow it. See the table in the [staff guide](03-staff-guide.md).

**I changed an event's date but it shows a different time.**
This is a known problem in the edit screen. See the warning in the staff guide.

**I cannot change an event's status. It says "Request failed (409)".**
Once an event is ON SALE or later, the edit screen cannot save changes. Ask the technical team to change the status.

**The scanner shows 000 checked in and 000 remaining.**
Your account probably does not have the gate staff or logistics role, or the device has never loaded the ticket list. Open the page once with internet using the right account.

**I cannot add costs as a coordinator.**
Only finance and managers can. Ask finance to add them.

**The dashboard revenue does not match the event report.**
The dashboard adds all paid bookings across every event. The report shows one event.

**Can I delete an event, a cost or an artist?**
Not from the app. Cancel the event instead. Ask the technical team for removals.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Booking | One purchase. It holds the seats and links to the tickets |
| Ticket | One seat with its own QR code |
| Tier | A ticket type: Early bird, Regular, VIP, VVIP |
| Hold | Seats set aside for a few minutes while you pay |
| Draft | An event that is saved but not public |
| Published | Visible to the public but not yet selling |
| On sale | Tickets can be bought |
| Sold out | No tickets left, set by the coordinator |
| Ongoing | The event is happening now |
| Completed | The event is over |
| Cancelled | The event will not happen |
| PAID, USED | Ticket statuses: paid and ready, or already scanned at the gate |
| Manifest | The list of valid tickets a scanner downloads for one event |
| Pending sync | Entries scanned offline, waiting to upload |
| Entourage | The people travelling with an artist |
| Audit log | The record of who did what in the staff area |
| EAT | East Africa Time |
